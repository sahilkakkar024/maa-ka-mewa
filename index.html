    Care & questions

    Good to know

    How much sugar is in the ladoos?

    The dry fruit ladoo comes in a regular version and no-added-sugar versions. Natural ingredients still contain some of their own sugar. Message us if you have a dietary question about any item.

    How long do they stay fresh?

    Roasted kaju and badam keep for about 6–8 weeks in an airtight jar. Ladoos and panjeeri are best within 3–4 weeks.

    Where do you deliver?

    We deliver locally and can courier to other cities. Message us your pin code and we'll share the delivery charge.

    Are these suitable for diabetics?

    The no-added-sugar ladoos have no added sugar, but they still contain natural sugars and carbohydrates. Please check with your doctor about portions.
